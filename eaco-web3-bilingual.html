1. Passive Income Pathways with EACO + SOL + DOGE + USDT + wBTC + wBNB (Solana) 一、EACO + SOL + DOGE + USDT + wBTC + wBNB 等 Solana 链上资产的被动收入路径

EACO is deployed on Solana. The pathways below combine EACO with SOL, Solana USDT and wrapped assets (wDOGE, wBTC, wBNB) to explore ecosystem yield. Yield is variable and carries risk. EACO 部署于 Solana 公链。以下路径将 EACO 与 SOL、Solana 版 USDT 及封装资产（wDOGE、wBTC、wBNB）组合，探索生态收益；收益不固定，存在风险。

DEX LP

Liquidity providing (EACO pairs)流动性做市（EACO 交易对）

Pair EACO with SOL, Solana USDT, wDOGE, wBTC or wBNB, add liquidity to a DEX pool, and earn a share of trading fees, plus possible token incentives. Watch out for impermanent loss.将 EACO 与 SOL、Solana USDT、wDOGE、wBTC 或 wBNB 组成交易对，加入 DEX 流动性池，赚取交易手续费分红，部分平台另有代币激励；注意无常损失。

Staking

SOL staking as a base layerSOL 原生质押打底

Stake SOL with validators for network issuance rewards. Part of the staking yield can be rotated into EACO to build an ecosystem position over time.将 SOL 质押给验证节点获取链上通胀奖励，再把部分收益置换为 EACO，逐步配置生态仓位。

Lending

USDT-SPL lendingUSDT-SPL 借贷生息

Idle Solana USDT can be supplied to DeFi lending protocols for interest; the interest can be used to accumulate EACO as a supplementary position.闲置 Solana 链 USDT 可存入 DeFi 借贷协议获取利息，利息收益可配置买入 EACO，作为生态补充仓位。

wDOGE

Wrapped DOGE combinationwDOGE 组合配置

Native DOGE cannot interact with Solana smart contracts directly; it must be wrapped as wDOGE before pairing with EACO in a pool, combining meme exposure with the EACO ecosystem.原生 DOGE 无法直接参与 Solana 智能合约，需封装为 wDOGE 后再与 EACO 组池，兼顾 MEME 资产与 EACO 生态红利。

wBTC

Wrapped Bitcoin (wBTC) collateral & pairs封装比特币（wBTC）抵押与交易对

wBTC brings Bitcoin exposure onto Solana. Use wBTC as collateral in lending protocols, or form wBTC/EACO, wBTC/USDT pools to pair Bitcoin's store-of-value role with DeFi fees. Verify the wrapping custodian and redeemability before use.wBTC 将比特币带入 Solana，可作为借贷协议抵押品，或组建 wBTC/EACO、wBTC/USDT 资金池，把比特币的价值存储属性与 DeFi 手续费结合；使用前需核实封装托管方与赎回机制。

wBNB

Wrapped BNB (wBNB) cross-chain exposure封装 BNB（wBNB）跨链配置

wBNB represents BNB on Solana via a cross-chain bridge. It can be paired with EACO or USDT in pools, or supplied to lending markets, giving BNB ecosystem exposure without leaving the Solana DeFi workflow. Check bridge reliability and wrapped-token liquidity.wBNB 通过跨链桥将 BNB 映射到 Solana，可与 EACO 或 USDT 组池，或存入借贷市场，在不脱离 Solana DeFi 流程的前提下配置 BNB 生态敞口；需核查跨链桥可靠性与封装代币流动性。

Ecosystem

EACO nodes & DAO contributionsEACO 生态节点与 DAO 激励

Run ecosystem nodes (community, AI-agent, service nodes) and earn EACO rewards; hold EACO to participate in DAO governance and qualify for ecosystem grants or airdrops.参与 EACO 生态节点（社区、AI-Agent、服务节点）运行，获取 EACO 激励；持有 EACO 参与 DAO 治理，争取生态资助与空投机会。

Portfolio

Balancing stable, blue-chip and growth assets稳健、蓝筹与成长资产的搭配

A conservative sleeve can hold USDT and wBTC for stability; SOL anchors the native Solana base; wBNB and wDOGE add ecosystem and meme exposure; EACO is the growth-ecology position. Sizing should match your own risk tolerance — this is a framework, not a fixed allocation.稳健层可配置 USDT、wBTC 平衡波动；SOL 作为 Solana 原生底层；wBNB、wDOGE 补充生态与 MEME 敞口；EACO 为成长生态仓位。仓位比例应与自身风险承受能力匹配，此处仅为框架，非固定配置建议。

Risk

Key risks to consider需要关注的核心风险

LP positions carry impermanent loss; staking, lending and node operation carry smart-contract and project risk. Wrapped tokens (wBTC, wBNB, wDOGE) add bridge, custodian and redeemability risk; token prices can be highly volatile. Never deploy funds you cannot afford to lose.LP 存在无常损失；质押、借贷、节点运行存在合约与项目风险；封装资产（wBTC、wBNB、wDOGE）另需承担跨链桥、托管方与赎回风险；代币价格可能剧烈波动，请勿投入承受不起损失的资金。
